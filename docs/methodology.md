# Analytical methodology

1. Acquire the UCI official ZIP, validate the expected workbook column list, preserve both sheets in source order and record SHA-256. Keep a hash-bound Parquet cache for reproducible faster reruns.
2. Cast to explicit types, preserve naive timestamps, trim keys and use decimal prices/integer line pence. Detect overlapping source coverage. The sheets contain exactly the same multiset of eight-field records for 1–9 December 2010 (22,523 rows per sheet); a blocking contract confirms identical values and multiplicities. Use the later-starting partition on overlapping days. Classify superseded rows as overlap_partition and retain them in raw/staging audit; core excludes them. Remaining exact repeats **within** authoritative partitions are ambiguous and retained with a separate deduplication sensitivity.
3. Normalize merchandise facts into dimensions, enforce keys/FKs and assert grain/reconciliation. Product regex is a policy, not verified category master data; inspect excluded non-merchandise codes before adapting this to a real business.
4. Exclude partial December 2011 from headline analysis. Compare January–November between years; source starts in December 2009. Build dense day-country scaffolding before rolling calendar averages.
5. Customer cohorts use first **observed** sale, which can be an existing buyer. A three-month warmup precedes mature repeat analysis; all customers also require 90 observable days. Neither warmup nor censoring correction creates a true acquisition cohort.
6. Cohort monthly retention has a fixed original-size denominator and zero-filled observed ages. Future cells are missing. Reorder intervals condition on a second observed order; they underrepresent long/incomplete intervals and are not a survival estimate.
7. Explore first-basket breadth and 90-day repeat. Report group counts/rates with 95% Wilson intervals; these express a Bernoulli working model, not the sampling design of a representative population. Month-standardize the binary 6+ versus 1–5 SKU contrast across common cohort strata, weighting by pooled stratum size. Plug-in independent-binomial variance supplies a descriptive normal interval with fixed stratum weights. There is no causal identification, and latent wholesale intent/customer history can confound the contrast.
8. Avoid unsupported statistical decoration: no randomized A/B result exists, no forecast is validated, and two seasonal cycles do not justify confident extrapolation. A future experiment plan is proposed separately from observed findings.

The ten executive findings are exploratory portfolio analyses; no selective significance claims or multiple-test p-values are used. Finite historical transaction totals are exact under the documented transformation policy. Statistical intervals apply only to descriptive repeat-probability contrasts under their stated model.

`reports/query_results/` contains the executed evidence. `reports/statistical_method.json` records the standardized contrast; `repeat_purchase_statistics.csv` records counts, rates and Wilson bounds. `reports/data_quality.md` records anomalies and disposition. `reports/run_manifest.json` and `query_plan.txt` contain measured execution evidence.
