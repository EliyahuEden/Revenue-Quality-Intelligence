## Future randomized CRM test (design, not executed)

Choose an ethically permitted contactable customer population with stable IDs and a declared eligible first purchase. Randomize at customer level, stratified by calendar cohort and pre-treatment basket breadth. Persist assignment, exposure, consent and exclusions before outcomes are observed. Primary outcome: second sale invoice within 90 calendar days; business secondary outcome: net merchandise value per assigned customer. Guardrails: cancellations, unsubscribes/complaints and discount spend (newly collected). Analyze intention-to-treat; pre-register exclusions and one primary hypothesis; plan sample size using a new-business baseline and a commercially justified minimum effect. Do not reuse this retailer's old rate as a guaranteed baseline for another company.

No discount-cost table exists, so neither ROI nor a profit-based launch decision can be estimated from this source. Historical gross purchases of dormant customers must never be labeled recoverable opportunity value. A commercial impact scenario would require explicit intervention, effect and cost assumptions; none is passed off as achieved impact here.
